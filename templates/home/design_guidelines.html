Design Guidelines

Design parts optimized for manufacturability. Review our guidelines to reduce costs, improve quality, and shorten lead times.

CNC Machining Tolerances

Standard tolerances follow ISO 2768 medium. We can achieve precision down to ±0.005mm for critical features.

View CNC Services

Wall Thickness

For CNC machining, minimum wall thickness is typically 0.8mm for metals and 1.5mm for plastics to prevent deflection.

Read More

Surface Finish

Standard machining finish is Ra 3.2μm. We offer polishing, bead blasting, and anodizing for Ra 1.6μm and smoother.

View Finishes

3D Printing Minimums

SLA/SLS resolutions can achieve layer heights of 0.05mm. Minimum feature size depends on the material.

View 3D Printing

Injection Molding Draft

Include a draft angle of 1-2 degrees on vertical walls for smooth part ejection. Sharp corners should be filleted.

View Molding

Accepted File Formats

We accept STEP (.step, .stp) as the preferred format. IGES, X_T, and PDF (2D drawings) are also accepted.
