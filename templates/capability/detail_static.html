Tolerances & Design Guidelines

As an ISO 9001 certified CNC turning company, we machine precision parts to meet strict dimensional requirements. Use the following guidelines when preparing your drawing or CAD file.

Service Tolerances
Max Part Size Up to Ø500 mm diameter × 1500 mm length
Min Part Size As small as Ø1 mm
General Tolerances Standard: ±0.01 mm
Precision: ±0.005 mm
Lead Time Standard: 3–7 days
Expedited: 24–48 hours
Design Guidelines
Radii Min internal radius 0.5 mm; larger radii improve tool access
Threads Standard sizes from M1.6 and larger; min thread length 1.5× diameter
Wall Thickness Min 0.8 mm for metals; 1.5 mm for plastics
Text & Holes Min text height 2 mm, depth 0.5 mm
Min hole diameter 0.5 mm (depth ratio 1:10)
Grooves & Slots Avoid extremely narrow grooves for stability

Materials for CNC Turning

Choose from a wide range of metals and plastics. We provide certified materials with full traceability to meet your specific mechanical and environmental requirements.

Aluminum

6061, 7075, 5052, 2024. Lightweight, corrosion-resistant, excellent machinability.

Stainless Steel

304, 316L, 17-4 PH. High strength, excellent corrosion resistance, ideal for medical & food industry.

Carbon Steel

1045, 1018. High tensile strength, excellent for structural and mechanical parts.

Brass & Copper

C360, C110. Excellent electrical conductivity, great for electronic components and connectors.

Titanium

Grade 2, Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V). High strength-to-weight ratio, aerospace and medical grade.

Alloy Steel

4140, 4340. Excellent toughness and fatigue resistance for heavy-duty applications.

ABS

Good impact resistance and machinability. Ideal for prototypes and enclosures.

POM (Delrin)

High stiffness, low friction, and excellent dimensional stability for precision gears and bearings.

PEEK

High-performance engineering plastic. Excellent chemical resistance and high-temperature stability.

Nylon (PA)

PA6, PA66. High wear resistance and toughness, great for bushings and wear pads.

PTFE (Teflon)

Extremely low friction and high chemical resistance. Used in seals and chemical processing.

Acrylic (PMMA)

Optically clear, excellent weather resistance. Used for display components and light guides.
